## Frequently Asked Questions

**My TV isn't showing up in the app.**
Make sure your iPhone and TV are on the same WiFi network. For DLNA TVs, tap "Scan for TVs" in Settings. It can take a few seconds for the TV to appear.

**The video plays silently on Chromecast.**
MKV files with AC-3, DTS, or EAC-3 audio tracks play silently on Chromecast — this is a Chromecast hardware limitation. Use DLNA instead for those files (your TV decodes the audio natively), or use a file with AAC audio for Chromecast.

**Subtitles aren't showing on my TV.**
Subtitles via Chromecast: place a `.srt` file next to your video with the same filename (e.g. `Movie.srt` beside `Movie.mkv`). The subtitle picker appears automatically.

Subtitles via DLNA are not supported — external subtitle delivery is ignored by most DLNA TVs. MKV files with embedded subtitle tracks will work natively on the TV.

**The app is asking me to subscribe but I already paid.**
Tap **Restore Purchase** on the subscription screen. If the issue persists, contact us below.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Open **Settings** → **Subscription** → **Manage Subscription**, or go to [apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions) directly.

**A file won't play locally.**
Castor uses VLC for local playback and supports virtually all formats. If a file won't play, it may be corrupted or use an unsupported codec. Try playing it in VLC on a desktop to confirm.
